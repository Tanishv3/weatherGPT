# SLIDE 2 — IDEA TITLE

## **WEATHERGPT**

### **Conversational AI for Real-Time Weather Intelligence**

### Proposed Solution

**WeatherGPT is an AI-powered conversational weather platform that converts complex meteorological data into simple, contextual and actionable guidance.**

### Core idea

**USER → ASK → UNDERSTAND → VERIFY → PREDICT → ADVISE → ALERT**

### What makes WeatherGPT different?

**1. Conversational Weather**

* Ask questions naturally:

  * “Will it rain tomorrow?”
  * “Is it safe to travel?”
  * “Will heavy rain affect my crop?”
* No need to understand technical weather terminology.

**2. Multi-Source Intelligence**

* Real-time weather observations
* Forecast APIs
* NWP models: **GFS / WRF**
* Historical & climate datasets
* Disaster warning feeds

**3. Action-Oriented Answers**
Instead of:

> “Rainfall: 62 mm”

WeatherGPT provides:

> **“Heavy rainfall is likely. Avoid low-lying roads and plan outdoor activities after the rainfall window.”**

**4. Indian-Language Accessibility**

* English + Indian regional languages
* Text + voice interaction
* Designed for users with limited digital literacy

### ⭐ Innovation

**WeatherGPT = Weather Data + AI Reasoning + Location Context + Early Warning + Decision Support**

### Bottom strip:

**One platform → One conversation → Multiple weather intelligence sources**
